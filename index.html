Services

Services

Dobato Nepal Private Limited is a professional services company. Work below is scoped by contract, delivered against a statement of work, and invoiced to the client.

Websites and digital systems

For schools, health posts/clinics, and inventory-based businesses. Reliable sites and simple operating tools at a stated price, with maintenance.

Education and learning services

Structured learning support, guidance materials, and educational content for schools and training organisations.

Health information (non-clinical)

Awareness and referral-oriented content for partners. Not medical care. Not diagnosis. Not prescription.

Research and advisory

Applied research and consulting on education, community health information, and related institutional questions.

Media for institutions

Digital content and video for organisations that need clear public information — not consumer advertising theatre.

Community and institutional advisory

Help organisations brief and work with local schools and health posts without substituting for those institutions.

Fees from this work fund company programs. Program participation by families is not sold as a package on this page.

Community programs

Community programs

Two programs only. Each has one promise. Neither replaces a school or a clinic.

Learning Catch-Up

For children already enrolled who are behind grade-level Nepali and mathematics.

  • Weekly practice sets via the partner teacher or volunteer
  • Dropout-risk flags shared with the partner school
  • Offline after a weekly sync; no student device required
  • Supports the classroom; does not replace it
Typical flow (design)
  1. 1. Teacher flags a child behind in class
  2. 2. Practice set matched to the gap
  3. 3. Monthly progress note to the school

Ask Before It's Urgent

Non-clinical guidance so a household can distinguish home-care from “go to the health post now.”

  • Delivered by a partner community health volunteer using approved scripts
  • Ends in home-care information or a named referral to the partner health post
  • Scripts reviewed by a licensed clinician before use
  • Dobato Nepal does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe
Typical flow (design)
  1. 1. Family contacts the partner volunteer
  2. 2. Volunteer follows the approved script
  3. 3. Home-care information or referral to the named post

Approach

How delivery is split

Company provides

  • Structured learning materials aligned to foundational literacy/numeracy practice and the local curriculum
  • Non-clinical health-information scripts and referral rules
  • Digital tools so the same script is used consistently

Always local

  • The teacher, health post, or volunteer who delivers
  • Language and pacing
  • Which ward is ready
  • Ownership of the outcome at the institution

Legal boundary

Dobato Nepal Private Limited does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. It does not replace a doctor, a health post, or a teacher. Every health interaction is information and triage toward a named licensed provider. Every learning interaction supports a real classroom. Volunteers are not presented as Dobato clinicians or Dobato teachers.

FAQ

FAQ

Are community programs charged to families?
No. Those programs are funded by the company. Institutional services are charged to the contracting organisation.

No. Dobato Nepal Private Limited is a registered private limited company.

No. We provide non-clinical information and referral to a named health post. We do not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

Not yet. Delivery starts only after a signed MoU with a local school or health post. There is no coverage map until that exists.
