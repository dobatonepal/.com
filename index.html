About Dobato Nepal

Where learning, care, and community meet.

Connecting people to the right knowledge, support, and opportunities to move forward.

Every life reaches a crossroads. A student deciding what to learn. A family looking for the right support. A community trying to stay healthy. A person facing a difficult moment and simply needing someone to stand beside them. Dobato Nepal exists for those moments. In Nepali, दोबाटो (Dobato) means a crossroads — a place where paths meet and a new direction can begin. That's what we're building: a trusted connection between people and the education, guidance, health support, and social care they need to move forward.

What We Do

We work at the community level, because meaningful change begins close to people — where their real needs, challenges, families, and aspirations are understood. Our work spans four interconnected areas.

Education

Creating opportunities to learn beyond traditional classrooms, develop practical skills, and build better futures.

Education Support

Helping students, families, educators, and institutions navigate learning, academic decisions, career pathways, and the next step forward.

Community Health

Bringing health education, prevention, wellness, and accessible community-based support closer to everyday life.

Social Support

Standing alongside individuals and families who need guidance, advocacy, welfare support, or a helping hand through difficult moments.

What We Believe

We don't believe meaningful change comes from simply delivering a service. It comes from building trust, understanding the community, and creating solutions people can actually use and own. That's why we listen first, work with local people and institutions, adapt to the needs of each community, and build for lasting impact — not a quick fix.

Our Approach

Global standards. Local hands.

Dobato Nepal isn't built around a single outside idea of what a village needs. It's built around a simple division of labour: we bring the knowledge that travels well, and local partners bring everything that has to stay local.

Think Globally

What we bring from the wider world

  • Structured learning materials aligned with recognised foundational-literacy and numeracy frameworks used in education systems worldwide
  • Health guidance and triage structured around internationally recognised primary-care and referral protocols
  • Lessons drawn from community health worker programs that have worked at scale in South Asia and beyond
  • Digital tools that keep the quality of guidance consistent, whether the first village or the fiftieth
Work Locally

What always stays in local hands

  • Who delivers it — a health post, a teacher, a community volunteer your family already knows and trusts
  • The language, tone, and pace it's delivered in, set by the community, never dictated from outside
  • Which village or ward goes first — decided by need and readiness on the ground, not by convenience
  • Ownership of the outcome — the goal is a community that's stronger, not a community that depends on us

“The idea can come from anywhere. The trust has to come from home.”

How It Reaches A Household

A trusted local partner first, technology second.

No app to download, no data plan required to start. A local partner already known to the community carries Dobato Nepal into homes that outside programs usually miss.

Dobato Nepal

Structured learning plans and health-triage guidance, built to internationally informed standards and kept current centrally.

Local Partner

A community health volunteer, teacher, or ward contact — already known and trusted — delivers it by voice, SMS, or in person.

Family

Gets a straight answer, a next step, or a referral to a real clinic or school resource — no data connection required.

The line we don't cross: Dobato Nepal does not diagnose or replace a doctor or teacher. Every health interaction ends in triage and a named referral, never a prescription. Every learning interaction supports a real classroom, never replaces one.
What We Run

Two programs, each with one narrow, honest promise.

We'd rather do one thing well in one district than everything, thinly, everywhere.

Education

Learning Catch-Up

For children who are enrolled but falling behind grade-level Nepali and math — the gap most programs miss, and government data shows is the single biggest driver of dropout.

  • Weekly personalised practice sets, delivered through the local volunteer or teacher
  • Early dropout-risk signals shared directly with the school, not just collected as data
  • Designed to work entirely offline after a weekly sync — no student device required
  • Built around recognised foundational literacy and numeracy benchmarks, adapted to the local curriculum
A typical journey
1. Teacher flags a child falling behind in class2. Weekly practice set matched to that child's gap3. Volunteer checks progress, reports back monthly
Health

Ask Before It's Urgent

A judgment-free way to ask a health question and know whether it's a home-care matter or a "go now" matter — before a small problem becomes an emergency.

  • Voice or SMS triage line run through a trained community health volunteer
  • Direct referral to a named partner health post — the handoff is tracked, not just the call
  • Plain-language health literacy content, reviewed by a licensed clinician before release
  • Built around internationally recognised primary-care triage principles, adapted to what's actually available locally
A typical journey
1. Family calls or messages the local volunteer2. Volunteer runs a short triage conversation3. Home-care guidance, or a direct referral to the health post
Global Foundations, Applied Locally

We didn't invent this from nothing — we adapted what already works.

Dobato Nepal's program design draws on established, publicly documented approaches from education and public health — reshaped for Nepal's specific realities, not copied in from elsewhere.

Education

Foundational Learning Frameworks

Grounded in widely used approaches to early literacy and numeracy assessment and remediation, adapted to Nepal's own curriculum and grade benchmarks.

Health

Community Health Worker Models

Informed by decades of evidence from community health worker programs across South Asia and Africa — and built to work alongside Nepal's own existing volunteer health network, not around it.

Delivery

Low-Bandwidth, Human-First Design

Designed for the connectivity Nepal's poorest households actually have — voice and SMS first — not the connectivity a head office assumes they have.

How We Sustain This Work

One platform, two tracks.

Our community programs are free, and always will be. Alongside them, Dobato Nepal also works as a registered professional services company — revenue from this work is what makes the free side possible. Here's what that looks like, in plain terms.

Media & Storytelling

Digital content, video production, and community reporting — built for organizations who want to reach people the way we do: directly, honestly, and without corporate polish.

Education & Learning Services

Structured learning support, guidance, and educational content for schools, institutions, and organizations investing in their communities.

Health Guidance & Awareness

Non-clinical health information and awareness work for partners building healthier communities — never a substitute for medical care.

Social & Community Advisory

Helping organizations connect authentically with the communities they serve, using the same trust-first approach behind our own programs.

Research & Professional Consulting

Applied research and advisory work on education, health, and community topics, for partners who need real answers grounded in real communities.

Every service we offer is registered and transparent, and connects back to the same mission: people first, community first, impact that lasts.

Questions

Common questions, answered plainly.

Is this really free, forever?+
Yes, for the families and communities we serve — that never changes. We're a community-centered platform, not a charity, and we're upfront about how we sustain that: through our own professional services (see "How We Sustain This Work" above), direct partnerships, and support from people who reach out to us directly. If you'd like to fund or sponsor a specific program, contact us by phone, email, or social media and we'll talk it through openly.
How do you decide which village or ward comes first?+
By need and by readiness — places with the largest documented gaps in access, where a trusted local partner already exists or is willing to take part. We deliberately start small and prove a program works before expanding.
How do you make sure health guidance is safe?+
Every piece of health content is reviewed by a licensed clinician before it reaches a family, and every interaction is designed to end in a referral to a real health post — never a diagnosis or a prescription from us.
Can I donate, volunteer, or partner with you?+
Yes to all three. We don't run a donation portal or a formal volunteer application — instead, contact us directly by phone, email, or social media (see the Contact section below), tell us what you have in mind, and we'll figure out the best way to work together. We're transparent about how any contribution is used.
Who runs Dobato Nepal?+
Dobato Nepal is a community-centered platform connecting people to education, health, and social support — built and run from Nepal, currently completing its formal business registration (see Trust & Governance below). We're growing from the community level toward a national and eventually global platform, one verified district at a time.
