DOBATO NEPAL

Site Editor

Update your homepage message, social links, articles, and videos — no sign-in, no code, nothing installed. Everything happens right here in this browser tab.

How publishing works
  1. Make your edits below.
  2. Click Download Updated Website — this saves a new index.html to your computer.
  3. On GitHub, open your repository, delete the old index.html, and upload the new one.
  4. Your site updates automatically within a minute or two.
01

Homepage Message

The headline and message people see first when they land on your site.

02

About Section

Your story, what you do, what you believe, and your vision — shown right after the homepage message, before anything else. This is usually the first place people go to understand what Dobato Nepal actually is.

03

Our Work

Show people what you've actually done — real work, with a photo or short video and a title. Upload straight from your phone or computer, no linking required. Photos are automatically resized so they don't slow your page down. Appears in the top menu as "Our Work", right after Programs. Hidden on your live site until you add at least one item with both a photo/video and a title, and turn it on.

04

Our Services

How you fund the free community work. Shown after "Global Foundations" on the live page. Keep each one short and professional — this isn't a checklist, it's a plain explanation of your revenue model.

05

Contact Info

Shown in a dedicated Contact section and used for the phone/email buttons visitors see. This is how people reach you to donate, volunteer, or partner — no forms, no portal.

06

Verified Local Contacts

Health posts, schools, cooperatives, and community volunteers you've personally verified. Only add contacts you've actually confirmed yourself.

07

Community Events

Health camps, education expos, and other events — especially ones that are free or low-cost for people who can't otherwise access them.

08

Articles & Updates

Post work updates, articles, or daily notes. Each one appears as a card near the top of your homepage, newest first. This section stays hidden until you add at least one and turn it on.

09

Social Media

Pick a platform, paste your profile link, and turn it on. Use up/down arrows to set the order icons appear in your footer.

10

YouTube Videos & Posts

Paste a link to a YouTube video, YouTube playlist, or a post from X, Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok — it embeds automatically. Hidden until you add at least one and turn it on.

Saves a new index.html — upload it to GitHub to publish your changes.
