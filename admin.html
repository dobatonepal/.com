Site Editor

Update your homepage message, social links, articles, and videos — no sign-in, no code, nothing installed. Everything happens right here in this browser tab.

How publishing works
  1. Make your edits below.
  2. Click Download Updated Website — this saves a new index.html to your computer.
  3. On GitHub, open your repository, delete the old index.html, and upload the new one.
  4. Your site updates automatically within a minute or two.
01

Homepage Message

The headline and message people see first when they land on your site.

02

Articles & Updates

Post work updates, articles, or daily notes. Each one appears as a card near the top of your homepage, newest first. This section stays hidden until you add at least one and turn it on.

03

Social Media

Pick a platform, paste your profile link, and turn it on. Use up/down arrows to set the order icons appear in your footer.

04

YouTube Videos & Posts

Paste a link to a YouTube video, YouTube playlist, or a post from X, Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok — it embeds automatically. Hidden until you add at least one and turn it on.
